# Black Friday 2026 and newsletter

All three Infiniti airbag products now use a $159.99 base on browser and server. Emblem extras remain $19.99 carbon / $79.99 LED. Persisted airbag cart entries recalculate with the new base.

The Black Friday window is November 11, 2026 00:00 through November 28, 2026 23:59:59 America/New_York. The end is exclusive November 29 00:00 EST. Dates appear in the announcement banner and airbag preview/card labels. After the window, promotion labels and wheel discounts stop; the updated $159.99 airbag base remains.

Custom wheels have an actual 10% discount, rounded once to cents and labeled 10% off. The prior genuine regular price is crossed out; no reference prices are inflated. Ready-to-ship presets and product-ID stock items are excluded. The server checks preset identity against its own fixed-build registry rather than trusting the browser readyToShip flag. The customer-requested misleading 15% label and artificial +5% original were not implemented. The owner subsequently approved “Up to 30% off” for the newsletter based on the existing airbag-cover discount from its stated $249.99 original price. The newsletter uses that headline and the company logo on desktop and mobile. The custom-wheel offer remains exactly 10% with unchanged genuine original prices.

Custom cards, featured cards, showroom selection, SEO landing pages and the configurator show the promotion; cart and checkout use the actual discounted amount. Original amounts are retained on cart items to prevent compounding a discount on reload. Product schema includes the current promotional starting price and expiration date. The Drift promo code was subsequently retired and is rejected by validation and checkout in any capitalization or surrounding whitespace. Other existing promo codes retain their established behavior.

The homepage newsletter popup appears after five seconds. It has desktop and mobile layouts, an explicit required consent checkbox, focus containment, Escape/close dismissal, seven-day dismissal suppression, loading/error/success states and successful-signup suppression. It sends subscriber data only when the visitor submits the form. It does not send any campaign emails automatically.

Newsletter routes: POST /api/newsletter/subscribe, POST /api/newsletter/unsubscribe and admin-protected GET /api/newsletter/admin. Subscribers persist separately from expiring saved-build leads. The startup migration records email, consent date/version, signup source and an unsubscribe token. Repeated opt-in updates consent without duplicating rows. Active subscribers can be exported from Admin → Sales → Newsletter subscribers, including per-address unsubscribe URLs that must accompany marketing emails. Export is authenticated and excludes unsubscribed entries; formula prefixes are escaped in CSV cells. Newsletter unsubscribe uses the existing /unsubscribe page with a newsletter-prefixed token. Privacy text includes the newsletter data use.

Validation: 29 frontend suites / 135 tests and 39 backend tests passed. Includes timezone boundaries, stock exclusions, noncompounding cart prices, all airbag prices, mixed checkout with custom/stock/airbag products, signup consent/storage, unsubscribe and protected subscriber export. Optimized production build and 44 SEO prerenders pass. Desktop and 390px mobile popup and airbag layouts checked in browser. No live payment or campaign email created during QA; no real subscriber was enrolled during testing.
