# Audi RS 2020+ source wheel and make compatibility

The Audi RS 2020+ configurator uses 44 original 2D WebP wheel assets and the source's calibration from https://build.miamicarbondesigns.com/#/car/audi-rs-2020. `public/models/audi-rs-2020/source/manifest.json` records each copied URL, size, and SHA-256 hash. The wheel includes Round, Yoke, Flat top & bottom, and Flat bottom shapes; grip material maps; carbon source masks; top rings; and the flat-bottom LED layer. The source does not expose paddle, lower-trim, or airbag-cover layers for this wheel, so those controls are absent here. Existing storefront color and carbon swatches remain in use.

The vehicle make dropdown offers Audi, BMW, Dodge SRT, Mercedes, Toyota, and Porsche. Audi and BMW lead to their current configurators. The other makes display an honest fitment request path until their own wheels are available; they cannot enter checkout using BMW or Audi pricing. The green compatibility label appears only for a selected style with a vehicle year/model matching the published family coverage. The current wheel photo remains required for final fitment verification. Audi RS 2020+ requires a 2020+ year and an RS model name for the label. Prices use the existing Audi B9 base and add-on rules.
